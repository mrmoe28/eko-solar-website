FAQ

Common Questions From Roofers

Do I need a special license to remove solar panels in Georgia? ▾
Georgia does not have a statewide solar contractor license, but DC electrical disconnect/reconnect requires a licensed electrician. If your crew touches the solar system without proper licensing, you expose yourself to liability and may void warranties. EKO Solar is fully licensed and insured for this work.
What happens if my crew damages a panel? ▾
If your roofing crew damages a panel, you (or your insurance) are typically liable. When EKO Solar handles removal, we carry $2M liability coverage specifically for solar equipment. We store panels safely and insure them during the project.
How fast can you coordinate with our roofing schedule? ▾
Typical turnaround is 48 hours from removal to reinstall, but we work around your timeline. We can remove panels the day before your crew starts and reinstall the day after your roof inspection passes. Rush and weekend scheduling available.
Do you handle the Georgia Power reconnection? ▾
Yes. Georgia Power requires a re-inspection after any physical system modification before grid reconnection. We handle the inspection scheduling, documentation, and utility coordination. This is included in our standard service.
What areas in Georgia do you serve? ▾
Primary service area is Metro Atlanta (Fulton, Gwinnett, Cobb, DeKalb, Forsyth). We also serve Athens, Gainesville, Macon, and Savannah by appointment. Travel fees apply outside Metro Atlanta.
Can we white-label your service to our customers? ▾
Yes. We offer co-branded proposals where EKO Solar appears as your official solar partner. You maintain the customer relationship; we handle the technical work behind the scenes. Ask about our partner referral program.
